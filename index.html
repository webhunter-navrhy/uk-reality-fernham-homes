The Chiddingstone

The Chiddingstone is a 4-bedroom detached home extending to 1,350 sqft, offering well-balanced living space designed for modern family life.

On the ground floor, the open-plan kitchen and dining area creates a bright and sociable hub of the home, with doors opening directly onto the Indian Sandstone patio and turfed rear garden, allowing for effortless indoor-outdoor living. A separate living room provides a comfortable space to relax or entertain, while a dedicated study at the front of the house helps to clearly separate home and work life.

A utility room, cloakroom and additional storage maximise practicality and ensure everyday organisation.

Upstairs, the principal bedroom benefits from fitted wardrobes and a contemporary ensuite. Bedrooms two and three are both generous doubles, while bedroom four offers flexibility as a nursery, guest bedroom or home office.

This home benefits from a single carport with loft storage and driveway parking.

Living at Blean

Positioned between the historic city of Canterbury and the Kent coastline at Whitstable, Blean offers a lifestyle shaped by nature, connectivity and everyday convenience.

Set beside ancient woodland and surrounded by scenic walking and cycling routes including The Crab and Winkle Way, the area combines peaceful countryside living with easy access to vibrant coastal and city destinations. Whitstable's independent restaurants, harbour and beaches are just a short drive away, while Canterbury offers renowned schools, cultural attractions, shopping and high-speed rail connections to London St Pancras in under an hour.

At the heart of the collection, landscaped open space, ponds and thoughtfully designed green areas create an environment designed to feel calm, spacious and connected to the surrounding landscape.
